📋 MEXT — How to Apply (Embassy Route, Recommended)

1
April

Check Japanese Embassy India website for application form

2
Collect Docs

Transcripts, degree cert, research plan (2 pages), passport, medical cert, CGPA conversion

3
Submit

Submit at nearest Japanese Embassy/Consulate

4
Exam

Written exam (Math + English + Japanese optional)

5
Interview

Interview at Embassy → Embassy nominates → MEXT approves → University placement → Visa

📋 MEXT — University Route (Higher Success)

1
Find Professor

Search Google Scholar + university lab pages. Target: U of Tokyo, Tokyo Tech, Osaka U, Kyoto U

2
Email Professor

Send CV, research plan (how your AI work connects to their lab), GitHub links. NOT mass template.

3
Get Acceptance

If professor agrees → they give "Letter of Acceptance" → Apply MEXT through university → Approved

💡
Email template: Subject: "Prospective Research Student — AI Agents / [Professor's Topic]" — Introduce yourself (2 lines) → mention their specific paper you read → explain your AI projects → ask if they accept students → attach CV + GitHub

📋 DAAD — How to Apply

1
Create Account

Register on daad.de portal. Choose "Development-Related Postgraduate Courses" or "Study Scholarships for Graduates"

2
Find Program

Find eligible DAAD-partnered Master's program in CS/AI

3
Prepare

CV (Europass format), motivation letter (1-2 pages), research proposal, 2 academic references, transcripts, IELTS/TOEFL, publications list

4
Submit by Oct

Submit online → Committee reviews (3-6 months) → Results March–April → If selected: admission + visa

What Committees Actually Look For

Clear Career Goals

"I want to research AI agents for healthcare" — NOT "I want to study CS"

🚀

Evidence of Initiative

Your hackathons, Google Ambassador, freelance work = GOLD for scholarship essays

📝

Strong SOP

This is 50% of the decision. Generic SOP = instant rejection. Customize for each program.

📄

Research Potential

Even 1 research paper or poster makes you stand out massively from other applicants

🌍

Community Impact

How will you use this education to help others? Show social responsibility.

💻

GitHub Portfolio

Active GitHub with deployed projects proves you can build — not just talk about building
