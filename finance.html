Step-by-Step

How to Apply for Education Loan

1
GET ADMISSION FIRST

Banks require admission letter before sanctioning. Start bank research while applying to universities.

2
GATHER DOCUMENTS

Student: Admission letter, fee structure, 10th/12th marksheets, graduation marksheets (all semesters), passport, Aadhaar, PAN, photos, IELTS/GRE scores
Co-borrower (parent): Income proof (salary slips/ITR), bank statements (6 months), PAN, Aadhaar, property documents (if collateral)
Collateral (if applicable): Property papers, valuation report, title deed

3
APPLY TO 2–3 BANKS SIMULTANEOUSLY

Don't wait for one rejection to apply to another. Compare offers before accepting.

4
BANK PROCESSES

Document verification (1-2 weeks) → Collateral valuation (1 week if applicable) → Credit check of co-borrower → Sanction letter (1-3 weeks)

5
DISBURSEMENT

Banks disburse directly to university (tuition). Living expense portion to your account. Usually semester-wise.

6
REPAYMENT

Moratorium: course duration + 6-12 months grace → Then EMI begins → Total repayment: 7-15 years

🚨 Avoid These

Financial Red Flags

❌ Don't take ₹50L+ loan for average US university — ROI doesn't justify it

❌ Don't count on part-time job to cover tuition — it only covers daily expenses

❌ Don't take loan without admission — some agents scam students this way

❌ Don't ignore interest accumulation — interest runs during moratorium in some banks

❌ Don't borrow for living expenses if family can cover — borrow only tuition

❌ Don't choose university based on loan availability — choose on merit/ROI
